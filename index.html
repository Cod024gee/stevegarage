About Us

Nairobi's Trusted Mobile Garage

KF Garage was founded with a simple mission: to make vehicle care convenient, transparent, and professional for Nairobi's car owners. We saw the frustration of taking cars to physical garages — the waiting, the uncertainty, the disruption to your day — and we knew there was a better way.

Our team of certified technicians brings decades of combined experience to every job. We invest in the best diagnostic tools, quality parts, and premium detailing products because your car deserves nothing less.

Whether you need an emergency repair in Westlands, a full service in Kitusuru, or a luxury detail in Karen — KF Garage is just a booking away.
